┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎯 WHAT IS IT?                                                            │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

A visual security boundary (fence) that appears on your video when you click
the button. Shows where restricted areas begin and end.

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚡ 3-STEP ACTIVATION                                                       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   1. Open detection.html
   2. Load video (upload or demo)
   3. Click the ZONE/FENCE button in video control bar

   ✓ Done! Fence appears on screen

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎛️ BUTTON STATES                                                          │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   STATE: OFF (Default)
   ├─ Color: Gray (normal)
   ├─ Glow: None
   └─ Fence: Hidden

   STATE: ON (After Click)
   ├─ Color: Orange with glow (#FFB000)
   ├─ Glow: Bright orange shadow
   └─ Fence: VISIBLE with yellow boundary

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🔄 HOW IT WORKS                                                           │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   Click 1: OFF → ON
   └─ Button turns orange
   └─ Fence appears (yellow dashed box)
   └─ Console: "🔒 FENCING ZONE: ENABLED"

   Click 2: ON → OFF
   └─ Button turns gray
   └─ Fence disappears
   └─ Console: "🔓 FENCING ZONE: DISABLED"

   Click 3: OFF → ON (repeat cycle)
